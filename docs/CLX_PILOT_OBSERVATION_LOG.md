## Recording Rules

- Do not include tracking tokens, passwords, bank details, internal UUIDs, or unnecessary customer contact data.
- Record confirmed observations separately from opinions or suggestions.
- Link serious issues to the Incident Log.
- Do not treat an observation as permission to change production data.
