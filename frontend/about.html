About Campus Life Express (CLX)

CLX is a campus commerce and services platform built to simplify daily life for students, staff, visitors, and businesses across universities.

The Evolution from Lunch to Life

Originally established as Campus Lunch Express focusing primarily on food delivery, our vision grew as we listened to the daily needs of the wider campus community and local businesses. People on campus needed convenient meals, printing, supplies, electronics, laundry, urgent errands, and a trusted Student Marketplace for peer-to-peer sales.

In 2026, parent company Dandalin Sauki Ltd restructured and remodelled the brand into Campus Life Express (CLX) — a full-featured campus commerce platform powered by 5 balanced, interconnected pillars.

Our 5 Core Pillars

1. Food: Campus restaurants, cafeterias, and quick grills.
2. Shopping: Tech, fashion, books, and campus essentials.
3. Campus Services: Printing, laundry, salon, and device repair.
4. Student Marketplace: Moderated peer-to-peer buying and selling.
5. Delivery & Errands: Fast hostel-to-hostel courier dispatch.

Parent Company: Dandalin Sauki Ltd

Dandalin Sauki Ltd builds purpose-driven digital products that solve fundamental daily challenges across African commerce and communities. CLX represents our flagship campus initiative, engineered to empower student entrepreneurs, provide verified merchants and service providers with sustainable revenue channels, and give the campus community time back for what matters.

TRUST & SAFETY SYSTEM

Trust & Moderation Framework

Community safety, verified merchant standards, and transparent marketplace transactions are the bedrock of Campus Life Express (CLX). We implement a multi-tiered security and moderation approach:

Verified Campus Merchants

Every restaurant, store, and service provider undergoes campus identity and hygiene/business checks before listing.

Peer Marketplace Audits

Listings are screened for prohibited items and pricing integrity with dedicated safe campus exchange zones.

24/7 Campus Resolution

Dedicated support coordinators actively arbitrate delivery disputes, order missing items, and vendor concerns.
