Campus Commerce FAQs

Answers to the most common questions from campus customers, merchants, marketplace sellers, and delivery couriers.

What is Campus Life Express (CLX)?

CLX is an integrated campus commerce platform by Dandalin Sauki Ltd connecting meals, shopping, campus services (printing, laundry, grooming), Student Marketplace listings, and courier errands across universities.

How fast is campus food and retail delivery?

Most meal and convenience deliveries are dispatched by campus runners and completed within 20–35 minutes across hostels, lecture halls, and campus gates.

How do I list and sell items on the Student Marketplace?

Navigate to the "Sell on CLX" or "Marketplace" section, click "Sell an Item", upload photos, set your price and contact details. Our moderators review listings quickly before publishing to fellow students.

How do campus vendors receive orders and payouts?

Verified vendors receive instant order alerts with item breakdowns and delivery runner details. Revenue is reconciled and paid out according to the merchant schedule directly to their designated bank accounts.

What if my delivery is delayed or an item is missing?

Reach our Support Desk immediately via the ticket form, call +234 915 073 6638, or click the WhatsApp Desk button. Our team will resolve the issue with the vendor or courier immediately.
