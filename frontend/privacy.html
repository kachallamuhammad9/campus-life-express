Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 • Effective Across All Campuses

Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Campus Life Express (CLX), a flagship digital commerce platform developed and operated by Dandalin Sauki Ltd ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of all customers, staff, campus merchants, and errand couriers utilizing our services across university communities.

This Privacy Policy explains the nature of personal information we collect, how that information is utilized to fulfill campus orders and deliveries, with whom it may be shared, and the rights you retain concerning your data.

Information We Collect

To deliver a seamless campus experience across Food, Shopping, Services, Peer Marketplace, and Delivery Dispatch, we collect the following categories of data:

  • Account & Identity Data: Full name, email address, phone number, university/campus selection, identification number (where required for verification), and profile image.
  • Hostel & Delivery Details: Selected university campus, hostel name, block or room number, off-campus residence address, delivery landmark notes, and recipient contact numbers.
  • Order & Transaction Records: Purchased meals, retail items, requested printing/laundry services, marketplace listings, dispatch logs, payment references, order totals, and timestamps.
  • Communications: Inquiries sent to our campus support team, feedback submissions, and dispute resolution messages.
  • Technical & Device Data: Device model, browser type, operating system, IP address, and session identifiers collected automatically to maintain security and prevent fraudulent access.

How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected information exclusively for legitimate platform purposes, including:

Order Fulfillment Routing your meal and item orders to verified campus merchants and assigning campus dispatch runners.
Dispatch Milestone Alerts Sending SMS, in-app notifications, and status updates as your parcel transitions through our 5-step fulfillment pipeline.
Marketplace Moderation Verifying Student Marketplace listings to eliminate banned items, exam leaks, counterfeit merchandise, and fraudulent sellers.
Security & Auditing Preventing abuse, verifying campus merchant authenticity, and ensuring high-trust transactions.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We strictly respect your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade customer personal information to advertisers or external marketing brokers. Information is shared only with:

  • Assigned Campus Merchants: To prepare your food or fulfill your product orders (names and order line items).
  • Verified Campus Runners & Couriers: Delivery name, phone contact, hostel name, and room/landmark notes to complete doorstep drop-offs.
  • Licensed Payment Gateways: Secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment processing for online checkout transactions.
  • Law Enforcement & Campus Security: Only when strictly mandated by legal subpoenas or in response to serious campus safety incidents.

Your Data Rights

Every registered customer and merchant retains full rights over their personal information:

  • Access & Correction: Update your profile, phone number, preferred campus, and saved campus address directly via the My Account page.
  • Listing Removal: Edit or deactivate active items on the Student Marketplace at any time once sold.
  • Account Deletion & Data Purge: Request permanent removal of your account and associated transaction histories by contacting our support desk.
